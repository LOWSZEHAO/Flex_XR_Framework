Procedure — the validation panel

The CSM board's procedure screen, mapped onto what FlexXR actually has: the meter is one bar per step in the compiled graph, the list is those same steps, and the active row is the only one that opens to its full text. The foot carries the run's own facts — and gives up its space to a mistake when there is one, rather than pushing the instruction off the panel.

Complete — the session report

The one moment of a long session anyone remembers, and unlike the CSM board this one has real numbers to show: FFXR_SessionReport carries the score, the step count, every mistake, the hints taken and the elapsed time. The score goes in the ring because the ring was already going to be there.

Same panel either way. The ring always means the score, so colour and the eyebrow carry the verdict and nothing has to be re-learned — and a failed run gets one line that a passed run does not need, saying why it ended. In Exam, a step running past its Timeout Seconds ends the run here rather than escalating a hint, which is what makes the mode mean anything: Guided and Practice spend a timeout on help, and Exam spends it on the verdict.
